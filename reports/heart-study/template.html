01

A fair starting point.

One heart, 33 bounded parameters. Each agent writes a complete plan before execution. All controllers receive the same milk episode at each seed and attempt.

Inputs & memory

Only volume and starting temperature are revealed. Flow, foam texture, spread and mixing remain hidden. No-notes planning contains no past attempts; current-attempt reflections are discarded. Notes carries only its own replacement note.

Every client request was audited. Gateway-side routing or memory injection cannot be independently certified.

02

A shape with a notch.

Overlap and contour agreement are penalized continuously by foam inside the target’s concave regions. The reference passes; both previously passing ovals fail.

Scoring & regression

Quality = 100 × max(0, min(IoU, boundary F1) − 0.25 × mean foam density in hull(target) minus target). Quality must reach 78.26%, alongside the existing precision, contour and serving checks.

The heart has one concavity cell; its raw density now gives a graded penalty. IoU and contour still use a binary foam mask.

03

A small, capped study.

200 agent attempts, 50 optimizer evaluations. Two models, five matched seeds and ten attempts per arm. All 400 model calls fit within the $50 cap.

Cost & limitations

Plans use medium reasoning; reflections use low. Completion caps are 4,096 / 2,048 tokens. Strict structured output permits up to two format corrections. This run had zero format errors or rejected plans.

This stylized surface model measures simulator control. Five seeds do not establish robust generalization; there are no held-out shape results, model-weight updates or transfer claims.

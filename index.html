WORKSTATION CONTROLS

Make a latte.

Click the illuminated buttons on the equipment, or use the console.

1 · Grind
Grind an 18 g dose.
2 · Extract
Pull a 36 ml espresso into the cup.
3 · Steam
Start steaming. Press again at 55–65°C. Milk scorches above 75°C.
4 · Pick up
Lift the pitcher. Drag in Cup View or use WASD to position it. Q / E adjust height.
Space · Pour
Hold to pour. Tilt controls flow. Pour high to mix, then lower the spout to draw. Aim outside the rim and the milk spills.
5 · Serve
Finish the cup. Use New cup to reset.
Physics implementation

Rapier handles rigid-body gravity and collisions. Milk droplets follow ballistic trajectories into a circular surface-fluid grid with advection, pressure projection, and foam deposition. This is a real-time approximation, not PhysX or a validated 3D milk-fluid solver. No preset latte image or hidden target score is used.

The ▦ button drops a physical sugar cube onto the counter.
